# The Kot Massacre (1846)

## Quick Facts
- **Year:** 1846
- **Era:** The Rana Regime
- **Key Figures:** Jung Bahadur Rana, Mathabar Singh Thapa
- **Location:** Kot, Kathmandu
- **Significance:** Initiated the hereditary Rana rule, lasting 104 years.
