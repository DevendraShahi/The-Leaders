# The Insurgency Begins (1996)

## Quick Facts
- **Year:** 1996
- **Era:** The People's War
- **Title:** The Insurgency Begins
- **Key Figures:** Maoist leaders, Rolpa insurgents
- **Location:** Rolpa and Rukum districts, Nepal
- **Significance:** Launch of the Maoist armed rebellion demanding the establishment of a republic.

## Narrative
In 1996, the Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist) ignited an armed insurgency in the remote districts of Rolpa and Rukum. Citing systemic inequality, corruption, and a desire for a republican government, the Maoists began a decade-long conflict that would engulf the nation.

The insurgency employed guerrilla tactics, targeting government installations, police stations, and infrastructure. It attracted thousands of peasants and marginalized groups, gradually expanding its influence across the country. The conflict resulted in substantial loss of life, displacement, and a profound destabilization of Nepal’s political environment.
