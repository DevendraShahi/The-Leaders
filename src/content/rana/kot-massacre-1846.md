# Quick Facts

| Fact | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Event** | The Kot Massacre – violent palace coup that installed Jung Bahadur Rana as the de‑facto ruler |
| **Date** | 31 December 1846 |
| **Location** | Kathmandu Durbar, Kot (the palace armory) |
| **Key Figures** | Jung Bahadur Rana (architect), Gajraj Kunwar, Mathabar Singh Thapa, other noble families |
| **Outcome** | Ended chaotic aristocratic power‑struggles; inaugurated the hereditary Rana autocracy lasting 104 years |
| **Casualties** | Estimated 30‑40 nobles killed on the spot; many more imprisoned or exiled |
| **Primary Sources** | "History of Nepal" by D.R. Regmi (1965); Contemporary court chronicles; British diplomatic correspondence (India Office Records) |
| **Image Suggestion** | Re‑creation of the Kot interior at night, with shadowy silhouettes of armed guards surrounding a blood‑stained throne room |
