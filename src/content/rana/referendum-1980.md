# Overview

In **May 1980**, Nepal held its **first national referendum** under the **Panchayat system**, asking citizens to choose between **retaining the existing party‑less Panchayat** or adopting a **multi‑party constitutional monarchy**. The referendum, conducted amid **student protests** and accusations of **electoral manipulation**, resulted in the **Panchayat’s survival** by a slim margin, intensifying calls for democratic reform that would culminate in the **1990 People’s Movement**.
