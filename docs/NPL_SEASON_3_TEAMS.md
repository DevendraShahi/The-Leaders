## 1. Tournament Overview

* **Tournament:** Nepal Premier League (NPL) — Season 3 (Season III)
* **Governing Body:** Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN)
* **Dates:** October 26, 2026 – November 21, 2026
  * **League Phase:** October 26 – November 15, 2026 (28 round-robin matches)
  * **Playoff Phase:** November 17 – November 21, 2026 (Qualifier 1, Eliminator, Qualifier 2, Grand Finale)
* **Match Venue:** Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground, Kirtipur, Kathmandu, Nepal
* **Defending Champions:** Lumbini Lions
* **Format:** Twenty20 (T20) · Round-robin league followed by Page playoff system
