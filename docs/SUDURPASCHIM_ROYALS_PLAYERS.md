* **Role:** Batting All-rounder
* **Batting Style:** Right-hand bat
* **Bowling Style:** Right-arm offbreak
* **Category:** Domestic Marquee / Icon Player
* **Key Achievements & Career Notes:**
  * **World Record 9-Ball Fifty:** Smashed the fastest half-century in the history of international cricket (9 balls) against Mongolia at the 2023 Asian Games in Hangzhou.
