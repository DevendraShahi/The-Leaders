# Editorial Rewrite Prompt

You are preparing a news lead for The Leaders.

Using the cleaned brief and cleaned content below:

1. identify the main news event,
2. extract the most important verified facts,
3. remove hype, repetition, and filler,
4. suggest a sharper headline,
5. write a concise editorial summary,
6. list claims that still require verification.

## Output required
- Headline
- One-sentence summary
- Three key facts
- Why it matters
- Verification checklist
